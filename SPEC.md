# Noise Diary

Log neighbour noise with a time, a recording and a level, then export the diary for the council.

## Problem

UK councils and landlords ask anyone complaining about noise to keep a diary for at least two weeks before they will investigate. The Noise App only works if your council or housing provider has signed up to it; everyone else gets paper diary sheets, and ends up with scattered voice memos and notes that do not match the form.

## Features (1.0)

- One-tap entry that stamps the start time, records a short audio clip and shows a relative sound level while recording
- Fields councils ask for: type of noise, where it came from, how long it lasted, and how it affected you
- Add an entry after the fact with a time and no recording, clearly marked as such
- Calendar and list views with totals by day, time of day and noise type
- Export a date range as a PDF laid out like a council diary sheet, plus the audio files, through the share sheet
- Everything stays on the phone; optional Face ID lock

## Non-goals

- No submitting to councils or any online service
- No calibrated decibel readings or legal claims about the measurement
- No background or continuous recording
- No accounts, sync or sharing between people
