## Description

Your council has asked you to keep a noise diary before they will investigate. Usually that means two weeks or more of paper diary sheets, a pile of voice memos, and notes that never quite match the form. Noise Diary keeps it all in one place and hands you a diary laid out the way councils and landlords expect.

ONE TAP WHEN IT STARTS
Tap Record noise. The time is stamped straight away, a short audio clip starts recording, and a live meter shows how loud it is relative to other noise you have logged. Stop when you have enough, then fill in the details.

THE QUESTIONS COUNCILS ASK
Every entry records the type of noise, where it came from, how long it lasted and how it affected you: kept you awake, woke you up, disturbed your children, stopped you working, and more. Add notes about what you heard and who else heard it. Your usual noise type and neighbour are remembered for next time.

MISSED ONE? ADD IT LATER
Write up noise after it happened, with the time it started and no recording. These entries are clearly marked as added later, with when you wrote them, so your diary stays honest.

SEE THE PATTERN
Browse your diary as a list or a calendar. Totals show how many times it happened and for how long, broken down by day, by time of day (including night time, 23:00 to 07:00) and by type of noise.

EXPORT FOR THE COUNCIL
Choose a date range and Noise Diary creates an A4 PDF diary sheet: date, time started, time finished, type of noise, where it came from, how it affected you, and a signature line. The audio clips come with it, numbered to match the rows. Send them by email, save them to Files or print them, using the share sheet.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no uploads. Turn on the Face ID lock to keep your diary hidden from anyone else who picks up your phone.

PLEASE NOTE
The level meter shows a relative reading from your iPhone's microphone. It is not a calibrated decibel measurement. Noise Diary does not submit anything to your council or any other service; you decide what to send and to whom. It records only while you are on the recording screen, never in the background.

## App Privacy

- **Data collected: None.** Select "No, we do not collect data from this app."
- Audio recordings, entries, names and addresses are stored only on the device and
  never transmitted to the developer or any third party, so under Apple's
  definitions they are not "collected".
- Linked to the user: not applicable (nothing collected).
- Used for tracking: No.
- Third-party SDKs: none. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Age rating notes
